# A2 — Little by little

A spacious blue-ink drawing with apricot crayon warmth: a loose line gradually finds its shape as the visitor meets the tutors and explores the lessons.

## The idea
The obvious sketchbook is a collage of bordered cards and school doodles. This is an open illustrated spread: a centred rounded-sans headline, equally sized real portraits at opposite sides, blue handwritten-feeling curves between them, and clear horizontal subject rows. White, cornflower blue and pale apricot replace the garden studios' oat, serif and green palette. There are no paper folds, cellular patterns, stock faces or tutoring clip-art.

## Scroll story
1. A loose ink connector joins the inner portrait edges. Its visible loop settles into an open curve as the portraits pass through the viewport. “Send a little mark” sends a blue dot to the other portrait, with an answering apricot ring; faces and names stay still.
2. In Barney's lesson section the portrait's recognisable folded bend turns downwards through the actual centres of the four numbered steps. The fold gradually opens out; the last stretch settles beside Wrap Up into a complete ring. A travelling dot follows the curve itself and the filled current marker follows the reader's scroll. Approximate timings remain separate text, never a proportional timeline.
3. At contact that same bend relaxes into an open curve that physically meets both email buttons. A small completed ring sits on the curve. On phones it bows through the spare left margin, clear of names, emails and actions.

The signature is the loose-to-ordered transition: answer between two people, find the lesson steps, then join two contact routes. The unrelated subject, biography and review lines have been removed. Nothing must be operated to understand the offer. Separate GCSE and A-Level rate rows now sit immediately after Enquire and before the portraits; detailed phone pricing uses full-width rows.

## Motion off and weight
Reduced motion shows the complete settled drawing at the three meaningful stops, without travelling emphasis. The optional control changes ink colour rather than moving it. There are no ambient loops, no scroll-jacking and no delayed content. Play lasts 1.4 seconds; off-screen and hidden-tab work is suspended. Pause ink occupies reserved header space, and the play button has its own row after the portraits.

The standalone folder includes both real photos, locally bundled Nunito and DM Sans, and their licences. The SVG/CSS implementation has no libraries, CDN, canvas or build step. A2 now has a small dedicated input-scheduled engine for its three drawings.
