## Motion and controls

Native scroll scrubs each assembly, without hiding or moving any information. Each scene starts as its top enters the viewport and completes after travelling 40% of the viewport height, reversing on return scrolling. A 110ms exponential filter smooths wheel steps; a quintic tail finishes within 300ms of the last target change. Pieces retain their contained paths and quintic landing. The contact sequence builds the doorway, holds it closed briefly and opens both leaves over the last 40% of its scroll progress. Equal lesson shapes do not imply proportional timings. Pointer input lightly perturbs the portrait composition. The in-flow Rearrange the mosaic button works with touch, Enter and Space and blends decorative colours over 1.1 seconds. Pause motion freezes the current compositions and colour blend, with an accessible pressed state; Resume follows the current scroll position smoothly.

Reduced motion renders fully settled compositions, including complete stairs and the open doorway. Rearranging then changes colour immediately. One requestAnimationFrame loop handles all drawing, with cached geometry read together before canvas writes. No animation runs while idle; hidden tabs cancel drawing and off-screen scenes do not draw. Canvas pixel ratio is capped at 2. Without JavaScript all copy, anchor links and disclosures remain accessible, with decorative areas blank.
