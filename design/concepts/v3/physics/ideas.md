# Duo B ideas

1. **Aperture / chosen B1.** Obvious: a glass ball and rainbow prism. Instead, a flat, architectural optical bench: an apricot input beam passes thin lavender lens planes and resolves into two equal portrait windows; ink-blue geometric sans, porcelain white and lilac.
   The page is an optical instrument at human scale, with a ray that splits into subjects, traces the lesson and gathers at the enquiry.
2. **Wave Study / chosen B2.** Obvious: a sinusoid in a hero banner. Instead, the whole page is a generous pale rose-and-blue wave score: offset portrait medallions, deep aubergine rounded sans, and a ribbon that changes from wave to ordered data as you scroll.
   Three wide chapters alternate photographic warmth and precise rhythm; touch bends a quiet wave, and the contact ending settles it into a line.
3. **Orbital desk.** Obvious: space or floating planets. Instead, paper-white ellipses and paired portrait crops orbit a shared typographic centre, in denim and peach.
   Reject: circles risk recalling the existing glass sphere and tend to make mobile content indirect.
4. **Soft compiler.** Obvious: code blocks and terminals. Instead, loose coloured instruction marks organise into readable sequences between content sections.
   Reject as a whole concept: less direct optical character; borrow its data-in-motion idea for Wave Study.

Typography decision: the obvious warm premium site uses serif headlines on oat with dark green. Both chosen concepts use sans serif voices, blue/plum actions, and porcelain/lilac or rose/sky surfaces; no green or oat.
