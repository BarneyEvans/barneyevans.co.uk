# A2 — Little by little

A spacious blue-ink drawing with apricot crayon warmth: a loose line gradually finds its shape as the visitor meets the tutors and explores the lessons.

## The idea
The obvious sketchbook is a collage of bordered cards and school doodles. This is an open illustrated spread: a centred rounded-sans headline, equally sized real portraits at opposite sides, blue handwritten-feeling curves between them, and clear horizontal subject rows. White, cornflower blue and pale apricot replace the garden studios' oat, serif and green palette. There are no paper folds, cellular patterns, stock faces or tutoring clip-art.

## Scroll story
1. A loose ink connector runs between the inner portrait edges. As the opening moves through the viewport, the scribble settles towards a measured curve without moving the headline or names.
2. At subjects, the same line becomes a quiet guide beside the three open rows. Its changing geometry has the character of a drawing finding its form.
3. The introductions continue the curve between the people, routed along the empty outer margin on mobile.
4. In Barney's lesson section the line resolves around the four numbered steps. Recap, Live Teaching, Practice and Wrap Up receive sequential emphasis while the approximate timings remain separate text.
5. It becomes a small review underline, then a long relaxed underline beneath “Ready to get started?” above the equal contact routes.

The signature is the loose-to-ordered transition: one recurring drawing becomes more composed as the page progresses. “Make a little mark” offers a brief pointer/touch/keyboard spring response in the opening. Nothing must be operated to understand the offer.

## Motion off and weight
Reduced motion shows the complete settled drawing in every section, without travelling emphasis. The optional control changes ink colour rather than moving it. There are no ambient loops, no scroll-jacking and no delayed content. Play lasts 1.25 seconds; off-screen and hidden-tab work is suspended.

The standalone folder is approximately 0.52 MB including both real photos, locally bundled Nunito and DM Sans, and their licences. The SVG/CSS implementation has no libraries, CDN, canvas or build step. A2 shares A1's small scheduling engine but morphs the drawing rather than progressively stitching it.
