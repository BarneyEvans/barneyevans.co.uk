# Ideas — Duo A

1. **A1 — A good thread.** Obvious: a serif on cream with a decorative dotted line. Instead: plum rounded sans on pale lilac, huge loose embroidered loops that connect real portrait medallions, become a lesson seam, then knot at the contact.
A tactile textile composition: full-width lilac hero, raspberry stitching, peach and marigold soft patches; thread follows the native scroll without moving the reading order.

2. **A2 — Little by little.** Obvious: a scrapbook of outlined cards. Instead: clear cornflower-blue typography on warm white, generous asymmetrical drawing-room composition, a hand-drawn pencil line that turns from loose scribble to precise constellation-like geometry, then a confident underline.
A continuous illustrated sketchbook: faces in hand-drawn blue ovals, flat apricot/crayon circles and broad blue chapter washes; no paper folding or cellular growth.

3. **Colour conversations.** Obvious: random decorative blobs. Instead: two translucent pastel pools meet into a new colour between equal portraits, then separate into subject lanes.
Not selected: the light-field studios probably own a more compelling version of this.

4. **The quiet loom.** Obvious: a conventional timeline. Instead: vertical and horizontal strokes gently weave behind each section into a calm fabric.
Not selected: the regular matrix risks looking cellular and both chosen directions already provide better human anchors.

Selected A1 and A2 differ in composition and material: dimensional fabric/thread versus open drawing/ink. Both use rounded sans rather than editorial serifs and avoid forest green/oat.
