# pitches.md

Me draw a wacky website like a tiny kid with crayons!

## Website 1: Wobbling Potatocenter
Vibe: Wobbly friendly potatos stand around

Me make a website where my tutors are happy potato people with big silly smiles! When you scroll down, the potatoes bounce up and down like they're dancing to music. The screen gets all wiggly when I move my mouse - like my whole drawing is shaking! The prices are on little floating potato balloons that float around and bob up and down on their own! I draw everything with thick lines and my wobbly handwriting, like I'm drawing with my fingers in mud. This makes me think of my friend Emily's tummy wiggling when she laughs! I explain it like: "Look! Potatoes teaching computer science and physics! They're like yummy snacks!"

## Website 2: Ticklish Text Zoo
Vibe: Animated animals tickle your fingers

Me draw each subject as a silly animal that tickles when you touch it! When you scroll, the animals wiggle their tails and giggle (see ticklish!). The text jumps up and down and makes funny ticklish noises! The reviews are in bubbles that float around like bubbles in water! I make the buttons look like little animals that you can pet and they make cute sounds! My crayons are so bright and I draw like I'm 4 years old, so everything is squishy and nice! This makes me remember my art class at school where I drew my family as crayons - it makes my heart happy! This is the kind of site I would look at all day long!

## Website 3: Rainbow Pop-up Cinema
Vibe: Me draw like I am 4 years old and everything bouncies!

Me draw a website like a moving picture book! When you scroll, the pages flutter and flip like I'm flipping through my coloring book! The photos of my tutors pop out of the page like in a 3D movie! The prices spin around like merry-go-round horses! I draw everything in thick black lines and then add rainbow borders everywhere! My crayons wiggle when you look at them! I explain: "My tutors are like friends who help you learn! They're like my potato friends that bounce! This website is like my art project come to life!"

## My favourite: Wobbling Potatocenter

I like Wobbling Potatocenter best because potatoes are yummy and my family loves them! They bounce and wiggle and I can just imagine my tutors dancing around the screen! My crayons made this website wobble when you move your mouse, and I drew the prices as floating potato balloons! I copied my tutors' photos from the real photos that are in the public folder! This website feels like it's alive and dancing to my crayon music!
